Take 1

Xander Hudson writes romantic, cinematic songs that never land fully happy or fully sad. A jazz-trained guitarist raised in New York, he sings like a star and lives like everybody else: warm, easy, present. His songs chase a version of himself he hasn’t caught yet, then stop for the few minutes he lets himself have the good thing anyway.
