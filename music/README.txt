Song clips for the postcards go here.

- MP3, 20 to 40 seconds each, under 1 MB (128 kbps is plenty).
- Name them simply, e.g. elevator-girl.mp3
- Then put the path in content/merch.js on that song's line:
    audio: "music/elevator-girl.mp3"

Nothing plays until a visitor presses "Play the songs". After that,
whichever postcard comes to the front plays its clip.
